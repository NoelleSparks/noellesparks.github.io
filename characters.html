Recorded Persons

The following records concern individuals whose lives became connected to the events surrounding Lyshal and the Northern Frontier. Certain entries remain incomplete, restricted, or disputed.

Person of Record I

Nara

Healer’s Apprentice of Dorshyllwyn

A young healer’s apprentice raised in the western village of Dorshyllwyn. Nara is known for her careful observation, practical knowledge of medicinal plants, and persistent concern for those overlooked by others.

Age
Twenty-two
Origin
Dorshyllwyn, Kingdom of Lyshal
Occupation
Healer’s apprentice
Affiliation
The village healing house
Known for
Herbal knowledge, adaptability, and an inconvenient curiosity

Record status: Active. Additional information remains restricted.

Person of Record II

Anoumi

Protector of Dorshyllwyn

Nara’s oldest and most steadfast friend, Anoumi is known throughout Dorshyllwyn for her strength, tactical judgment, and refusal to leave the vulnerable undefended. Her humor is sharp, her loyalty unwavering, and her patience considerably greater with children than with fools.

Age
Thirty
Origin
Dorshyllwyn, Kingdom of Lyshal
Occupation
Member of the Royal Protection League
Family
Married; mother of Rayan and Tifawt
Known for
Combat training, tactical calm, and formidable loyalty

Record status: Active. Protective assignments partially restricted.

Person of Record III

Gideon

Royal Guard of Lyshal

A disciplined member of the Royal Guard whose composure and formal restraint conceal a deeply attentive nature. Gideon is accustomed to duty, scrutiny, and expectations rarely spoken aloud, though his loyalties do not always remain as simple as the Crown might prefer.

Origin
Kingdom of Lyshal
Occupation
Royal Guard
Affiliation
The Crown of Lyshal
Training
Weapons, protection, court protocol, and tactical defense
Known for
Composure, persistence, and careful attention to detail

Record status: Active. Portions of service history remain sealed.
