Regional Record I

The Edenaren Wood

No complete survey of the Edenaren Wood has ever been entered into the royal record. Its boundaries appear unchanged upon the oldest surviving maps of Lyshal, though the roads drawn toward it have altered repeatedly. Some vanish beneath the first line of trees. Others return travelers to the place from which they began.

Accounts gathered along the eastern border describe bark and leaves that catch the light in shifting colors—ruby, sapphire, emerald, amethyst, and shades for which the Royal Cartographic Office possesses no proper names. These reports remain disputed, as the same trees often appear ordinary when observed from a different position.

The Crown has commissioned seven formal expeditions into the Wood. None produced a reliable map of its interior. The most complete surviving journal ends three days after entry, though the expedition itself returned nearly a month later. Its members offered no explanation for the missing record.

Status: Interior survey incomplete. Unescorted entry is strongly discouraged.

Kingdom Record I

The Kingdom of Lyshal

Lyshal extends eastward from the border of the Edenaren Wood to the mountain passes of the northern frontier. Its oldest settlements were raised along the rivers and cultivated valleys that divide the forest from the highlands.

The kingdom takes its name from an elder word associated with the familiar light of evening—the warmth that settles over fields, windows, and well-traveled roads as the sun reaches the horizon. This meaning remains preserved in Lyshal’s royal seal, where a single sun is held within a crowned shield.

Though its central territories are heavily settled, much of Lyshal’s western boundary remains difficult to govern. Villages nearest the Edenaren Wood maintain customs older than many royal laws, and reports from those regions often reach the capital slowly, altered by distance, superstition, or deliberate silence.

The Crown continues to maintain the northern roads and mountain passes, though several routes recorded upon earlier surveys are no longer used. Travelers are advised to remain upon marked roads and seek shelter before the final light leaves the horizon.

Status: Recognized sovereign territory. Western records remain incomplete.

Settlement Record I

Dorshyllwyn

Dorshyllwyn is a small western settlement situated near the oldest surviving road between central Lyshal and the Edenaren Wood. Though absent from several early royal surveys, local records suggest the village has stood in some form for far longer than its official founding date would indicate.

The surrounding land is known for medicinal herbs, grazing fields, and gardens cultivated by households that preserve their own remedies through generations. Travelers seeking a royal physician rarely stop there. Travelers seeking someone who understands what grows beneath the hedgerows often do.

Life in Dorshyllwyn is governed as much by custom as by written law. Doors remain unbarred during daylight, neighboring families share winter stores when harvests fail, and children are taught from an early age never to follow unfamiliar lights toward the Wood.

The village maintains no formal western gate. Beyond the final cottages, the road narrows gradually until stone gives way to earth and the first distant trees become visible along the horizon.

Status: Inhabited rural settlement. Western boundary remains unsurveyed.
