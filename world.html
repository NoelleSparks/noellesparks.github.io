Regional Record I

The Edenaren Wood

No complete survey of the Edenaren Wood has ever been entered into the royal record. Its boundaries appear unchanged upon the oldest surviving maps of Lyshal, though the roads drawn toward it have altered repeatedly. Some vanish beneath the first line of trees. Others return travelers to the place from which they began.

Accounts gathered along the eastern border describe bark and leaves that catch the light in shifting colors—ruby, sapphire, emerald, amethyst, and shades for which the Royal Cartographic Office possesses no proper names. These reports remain disputed, as the same trees often appear ordinary when observed from a different position.

The Crown has commissioned seven formal expeditions into the Wood. None produced a reliable map of its interior. The most complete surviving journal ends three days after entry, though the expedition itself returned nearly a month later. Its members offered no explanation for the missing record.

Status: Interior survey incomplete. Unescorted entry is strongly discouraged.
