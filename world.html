Regional Record I

The Edenaren Wood

No complete survey of the Edenaren Wood has ever been recorded. Its borders have remained largely unchanged through the memory of kingdoms, though roads approaching the forest seldom continue beyond the first line of trees.

Status: Survey incomplete.

Kingdom Record I

The Kingdom of Lyshal

Lyshal occupies the lands east of the Edenaren Wood, extending from the northern mountain passes into the cultivated valleys of the southern frontier.

Further records awaiting classification.

Settlement Record I

Dorshyllwyn

A small settlement situated near the western roads of Lyshal, Dorshyllwyn lies closer to the Edenaren Wood than most villages marked upon official royal surveys.
